Data checks

    Settings

    Location for temperatures

    Temperatures come from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). Only the coordinates are sent.

    Degree days and forecast

    Both are outdoor daily average temperatures, not thermostat settings. A day at or below the threshold counts (base − temperature) degree days, otherwise 0. The defaults 18 °C and 15 °C follow the Eurostat method and suit a thermostat around 20 °C.

    Data format

    For creating or editing data files by hand. The schema describes every field and its limits; the example is a small valid file you can open, copy or import. Importing it replaces your current data, so export first.
